Research Projects

Aging of the Primate Adrenal Gland

Single-cell transcriptomic atlas of young and old cynomolgus monkey (Macaca fascicularis) adrenal glands, paired with bulk RNA-seq. The pipeline covers QC, doublet removal, integration, cell-type annotation, age-related differential expression, transcription-factor regulon analysis, pseudotime trajectories, cell–cell communication and gene-set scoring.

  • Seurat
  • DESeq2
  • SCENIC
  • Monocle
  • CellPhoneDB

Exercise and the Mouse Multi-Tissue Transcriptome

Single-cell, single-nucleus and bulk RNA-seq analysis of mouse tissues in an exercise study. Parallel workflows in Seurat (R) and Scanpy (Python) handle read mapping, QC, doublet detection, batch integration, cell-type annotation, differential expression, regulon inference and ligand–receptor analysis.

  • Python
