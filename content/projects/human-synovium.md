Single-nucleus transcriptomic profiling of shoulder synovium from young and aged donors, with me as a
co-first author. We described lining and sublining stromal cell populations and found that loss of the
transcription factor FOXO1 drives their senescence, which was confirmed in FOXO1-depleted human stromal
cells.
