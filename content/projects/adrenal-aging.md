A single-nucleus transcriptomic atlas of young and aged cynomolgus monkey adrenal glands, paired with
bulk RNA-seq. I was responsible for the bioinformatics analysis as a co-first author. We found that the
zona reticularis, which makes the hormone DHEA-S, is especially vulnerable to aging, and that loss of the
cholesterol receptor LDLR lowers hormone production.
