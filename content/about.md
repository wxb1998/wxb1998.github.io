I am a Ph.D. student in Human Genetics at the University of Pittsburgh School of Public Health, working in the lab of Dr. Radosveta Koldamova and Dr. Iliya Lefterov. I use single-cell multi-omics, mainly single-nucleus RNA-seq and ATAC-seq, to study how gene regulation changes in the brain in Alzheimer's disease and aging.

Before coming to Pittsburgh, I received my master's degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I did my research at the Institute of Zoology and helped build single-cell atlases of aging in primate, mouse and human tissues. This work was published in *Nature*, *Nature Aging*, *Protein & Cell* and *The Innovation*. From January to July 2024, I worked as a bioinformatics engineer in Dr. Xuetao Cao's group at Changping Laboratory in Beijing, focusing on single-cell multiome analysis.

My long-term goal is a research career that combines data science and biology, to understand the genetic and non-genetic factors that shape human aging and disease.
